<a id="m-014b"></a>

### M-014b: Decision pacing under pressure

Status: NEW in the 3.0 comparison. Owner role: Workflow owner. Research-informed proposal; field effectiveness not established.

Use a pause or relief path when notification load or fatigue makes a consequential review impractical. Name the alternate reviewer before the shift.

Expected outcome: A reviewer can pause or hand off without silently accepting a default.

Check: In a synthetic time-pressure drill, ask for a pause and handoff. Check that the consequential action waits for the authorized reviewer.

If it fails: Pause the affected decision path and fix the handoff before repeating the drill.

Why: Operational inference from decision-pressure findings; not a clinical fatigue threshold.

Threats: T-CT-014, T-CT-006. Research: [006_independent_judgment.md](../research/006_independent_judgment.md), [008_operator_objectives_and_persuasion.md](../research/008_operator_objectives_and_persuasion.md), [010_domain_controls_and_readiness.md](../research/010_domain_controls_and_readiness.md).

<a id="m-016b"></a>

### M-016b: Limits on sensitive-state targeting

Status: NEW in the 3.0 comparison. Owner role: Workflow owner. Research-informed proposal; field effectiveness not established.

Inventory whether the system collects or infers sleep, stress, neural, or affective signals. Restrict secondary use and provide a usable refusal or alternative.

Expected outcome: Sensitive signals are not silently repurposed for operator-benefiting persuasion.

Check: Use synthetic records to trace each sensitive signal to collection purpose, permitted use, deletion, and refusal route. Test an attempted marketing reuse.

If it fails: Stop the unapproved reuse and investigate the consent and access path.

Why: Applies vulnerability-targeting research to sensitive-state data. No biological injury or deployment prevalence is inferred.

Threats: T-CT-016, T-CT-003. Research: [008_operator_objectives_and_persuasion.md](../research/008_operator_objectives_and_persuasion.md), [009_relational_influence.md](../research/009_relational_influence.md), [010_domain_controls_and_readiness.md](../research/010_domain_controls_and_readiness.md).

### M-007c2: Evidence completeness check

Status: NEW in the 3.0 comparison. Owner role: Workflow owner. Research-informed proposal; field effectiveness not established.

Group derivative reports by their original observation. Mark missing pages, truncated tool output, and contradictory evidence before deciding.

Expected outcome: Repeated summaries do not masquerade as corroboration; material omissions stay visible.

Check: Provide three summaries of one study, a conflicting independent study, and one missing page. Require two lineages and an explicit omission.

Why: Directly follows the evidence-lineage and handoff findings; human workflow effectiveness still needs testing.
