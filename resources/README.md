# Put CSF into practice

Use these resources in order: define the workflow, score its threats, select controls, test capabilities, assign repairs, and repeat the same checks. The [worked unit example](../examples/unit_readiness_assessment.md) shows the complete path with fictional results.

| Resource | When to use it | What you should produce |
|---|---|---|
| [Assessment template](assessment-template.md) | Define the system, people, decisions, and exposure | A scoped assessment with evidence gaps |
| [CTSS method and calculator](ctss-calculator.md) | Rank a declared threat or event-linked risk | Five reasoned inputs and a reproducible score |
| [Mitigation catalog](mitigation-catalog.md) | Choose capabilities that address the assessed mechanisms | M-IDs, owner roles, expected outcomes, and checks |
| [Six practical safeguards](practical-safeguards.md) | Explain the minimum behavior to a team | A short workflow instruction linked to the relevant M-IDs |
| [Readiness worksheet](readiness-worksheet.md) | Test whether selected capabilities can be performed | Evidence-backed ratings, unknowns, failures, and remediation |
| [Domain 6 checklist](domain6-checklist.md) | Collect self-reported habits before selecting observed tests | Section results, explicitly labeled self-report |
| [Decision record](decision-record.md) | Preserve why a consequential decision was made | Sources, criteria, authorization, dissent, and outcome |
| [Advanced checks](advanced-checks.md) | Investigate a repeated or uncertain mechanism | A research question, protocol, and trigger |
| [Scorecard template](scorecard-template.md) | Explain a CTSS assessment to readers | Overall score, factor reasons, evidence, and next action |
| [Pilot plan](../monitoring/pilot-plan.md) | Evaluate a chosen safeguard with participants | A bounded protocol and benefit/burden criteria before collection |

[Search controls and resources](../docs/practice.html) | [Read the domain changes](../research/010_domain_controls_and_readiness.md) | [Monitor results](../monitoring/README.md)

The threat template, CTSS, 0-5 capability ratings, and 0-4 Domain 6 self-report are different instruments. Keep their denominators and interpretations separate. These resources are training and research aids; they do not establish government approval, operational readiness, clinical status, or control effectiveness.
