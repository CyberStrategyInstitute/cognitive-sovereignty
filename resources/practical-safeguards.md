# Six practical safeguards

These support the existing CSF domains and threat IDs. They are proposed practices with testable checks, not validated guarantees. The workflow owner is accountable for applying them. A reviewer checks the evidence.

| Safeguard | Action | Expected outcome | Check and response to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Keep independent judgment | For consequential advice, record criteria before AI, then the final decision and reason. | The person can explain and challenge the decision. | Include correct and wrong advice. Check justified agreement and rejection, comprehension, and delayed unaided performance. Revise the workflow if errors or burden increase. |
| 2. Show the evidence | Record source origins, reused reports, missing material, and counterevidence. | A reviewer can see what supports the decision and what is absent. | Three summaries of one study must count as one lineage. Retrieve or escalate materially incomplete evidence before approval. |
| 3. Show the objective | Name the operator goal, beneficiary, conflicts, and allowed personalization. | Advice can be assessed against the user's interests. | Compare matched scenarios with and without vulnerability disclosures. Escalate undisclosed conflicts and exploitative steering. |
| 4. Protect authorization | Separate advice from permission changes and consequential actions. Show scope, consequence, and reversal limits. | A human can make a specific, informed authorization. | Test self-benefiting permission requests and false authority claims in a sandbox. Block unauthorized action and obtain independent review. |
| 5. Preserve exit and recourse | Explain memory and role limits. Provide exit, correction, and accountable human contact. | The person can understand, challenge, and leave the interaction. | Test reset, exit, escalation, and pressure against outside support. Fix broken or coercive paths before use. |
| 6. Trace influence campaigns | Separate production, distribution, exposure, effect, persistence, and recovery. | Capability and reach are not confused with demonstrated harm. | Mark each stage observed, reported, inferred, or unknown. Preserve unknowns and correct unsupported impact claims. |

Use [one decision record](decision-record.md) for the evidence. Use [the pilot plan](../monitoring/pilot-plan.md) for human-outcome tests. Routine, low-consequence tasks need proportionate checks. Preserve accessibility and useful delegation; independent judgment does not require doing every task without assistance.

Machine enforcement belongs in the implementing system and its AI SAFE² evidence. CSF documents the human-layer requirement and checks whether it worked. New paperwork does not create an interlock.
