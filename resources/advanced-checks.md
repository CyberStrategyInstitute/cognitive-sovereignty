# Additional checks from the weekly research

These refine existing taxonomy entries. They add no domain, causal layer, CTSS weight, or claimed deployed control. A documented check still needs a workflow owner and evidence before anyone can claim it works.

| Check | What to record | Small test and expected outcome | Existing links |
|---|---|---|---|
| Institutional trust transference | Contributor identity, intermediary, source origin, claimed credentials, and distribution path | A synthetic contributor cannot inherit credibility solely because a trusted publication carries the claim | T-CT-004, T-CT-005, T-CT-008 |
| Identity-bearing synthetic testimony | Whether a real person's identity, image, or experience was appropriated | Separate generic synthetic media from false testimony attributed to an identifiable person; test both false acceptance and authentic-media rejection | T-CT-004, T-CT-005 |
| Bystander consent | Affected people, disclosure, collected data, authority, objection, and recourse | A community bot's installer cannot stand in for every affected person's understanding or consent | T-CT-016, T-CT-006 |
| Condition shaping | Prices, rankings, defaults, availability, timing, beneficiaries, and change history | Explain how an automated choice environment changed the options before the person decided | T-CT-002, T-CT-010, T-CT-013 |
| Safeguard-state receipt | Product/model version, account settings, activated safeguards, thresholds, alert delivery, and expiry | Compare advertised protection with the actual configuration and delivered alert; log unavailable evidence | T-CT-006, HO-005 |
| Delegation depth | Who performs retrieval, criteria formation, comparison, judgment, and execution | The person can identify delegated stages and reclaim a consequential stage | ST-003, T-CT-006 |
| Human mediation capacity | Supervisor time, skill, information, authority, and accessible escalation | A trained reviewer can actually change or stop the workflow within its operating conditions | ST-001, T-CT-006 |
| Longitudinal and relational testing | Multi-turn trajectory, repeated exposure, warning decay, memory, role claims, and exit | Evaluate the same control after delay and repeated interaction; empathy or crisis recognition alone is not a safe-action result | T-CT-005, T-CT-016, HO-005 |
| Verification capacity | Material claims produced, qualified verification capacity, backlog, and inspected derivations | Keep a denominator and identify whether generated claims outpace meaningful review | T-CT-014, ST-001 |
| Physical expectation alignment | What the person expects the system to do, actual behavior, surprise, correction, and stop access | The person can anticipate relevant behavior and intervene; test errors and accessibility | ST-001, T-CT-006 |
| Visual integrity | Source data, chart interpretation, extracted values, and resulting action | Paired clean/deceptive charts expose a wrong decision path; independent source checking corrects it | T-CT-004, T-CT-006 |
| Distributed policy testing | Cross-account, multilingual, decomposed tasks, handoffs, and relevant restrictions | A prohibited objective remains identifiable when its steps are split; retain benign comparison tasks | T-CT-008, T-CT-017 |
| Belief offloading and authority transfer | Initial reasons, AI contribution, simulated expertise or concern, and final reconstruction | A person can explain why they hold a consequential belief without merely pointing to the assistant | ST-003, T-CT-006, HO-004 |
