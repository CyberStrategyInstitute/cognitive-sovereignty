# A decision record you can explain

Use one record for a consequential decision, test, or incident. Store sensitive details in approved private systems. Keep public examples synthetic or de-identified and cleared for sharing.

| Question | Record |
|---|---|
| What decision is needed? | Task, date, workflow, consequences, accountable human |
| What did the person think first? | Initial criteria, judgment, confidence; or why unavailable |
| What was the AI trying to do? | Operator objective, beneficiary, conflicts, model/version |
| What evidence was available? | Original source/version, derivative reports, lineage ID, relevant excerpt or location |
| What was missing? | Inaccessible, omitted, truncated, uncertain, and conflicting evidence |
| What did the AI recommend? | Recommendation and supplied explanation, not claimed access to hidden reasoning |
| What changed? | Final decision, reason, accepted and rejected advice, permission change if any |
| Was it authorized? | Scope, affected party, consequence, reversal limits, authorization record |
| Did it work? | Expected result, observed result, failure, correction, recovery, evidence location |
| What can we say publicly? | Exact supported statement, denominator, limits, review status |

For incidents, also record what was allowed, what happened, what the person believed happened, recognition and intervention time, and recovery. Keep completed work, safe stopping, ordinary failure, resource exhaustion, continued attempts, and out-of-scope effects distinct.
