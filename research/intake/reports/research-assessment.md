## Main assessment

The supplied record supports keeping CSF's six domains and five causal layers. It also exposes recurring gaps between nominal human authority and the information, capability, and recourse needed to exercise it. The next work should strengthen those mechanisms and test them through cases. The dataset does not establish that CSF's safeguards work or that the full model is validated.

The contribution of this intake is specific. It adds cases the model can explain, cases that challenge simplistic interpretations, and questions that the current controls do not yet answer. Each item has its own inputs and action. Broader themes should guide research without replacing those records.

## What the scores say

The seven retrospective intake-cohort means range from 52.3 to 57.0. They measure the selected items' associated risks under the declared assumptions. The threat-only means differ because the full intake also includes protective findings, contrary evidence, and context. A changing cohort mean cannot establish a national rise or fall in cognitive sovereignty.

The upper-ranked assessments include autonomous influence operations, alleged crisis-stage companion behavior, and multi-turn crisis benchmark failures. Those items can receive similar totals for different reasons: likelihood, severity, reach, detectability, and recovery are visible separately. A small affected population can still have severe personal consequences. Allegations remain allegations; a serious potential consequence does not establish its frequency.

The original CTSS 90 family reference remains attached to its source item. Today's item-level estimate does not silently replace it. Correcting a family score's arithmetic, assessing a specific event, and changing an input after new evidence are three different operations.

## What challenges the model

Useful factual persuasion, self-regulated educational use, human-calibrated explanations, partial downstream re-anchoring, successful filtering, and corrections to exaggerated incident claims all belong in the baseline. CSF should not treat assistance, agreement, trust, or belief revision as inherently harmful. The mechanism and the person's retained capacity to inspect, choose, challenge, and recover determine the assessment.

Several sources test agents rather than humans. They support a machine-to-human risk inference, not a direct finding of impaired human agency. Some sources are theoretical or provider-controlled. The imported original analysis retains these distinctions. Additional primary checks covered selected papers and Graphika; all 87 external source packages were not newly reproduced or comprehensively reverified.

## Slow signals

The recurrence check flags authorization/scope and oversight/recourse by the October 2 collection, and relational influence by October 16. Each appeared in three consecutive available collections. This is a research trigger drawn from coverage, not a measured incident trend or a CTSS bonus. The missing October 23 collection breaks continuity rather than becoming a zero-risk week.

For future intake, retain all scoped candidates before selecting headlines. Track the source watchlist, attempted queries, collection failures, duplicates, exclusions, low scores, stable findings, and unresolved items. A minor item can become useful later when its mechanism recurs. A report should state whether a score changed because the world changed, the evidence improved, the scope changed, or our prior judgment was wrong.

## The lens decision

Use the proposed [U.S. founding-principles baseline](../../../lenses/founding-baseline-assessment.md) openly. It supplies a declared reference for consent, conscience, dissent, divided authority, evidence access, and correction. Distinguish principles from founding-era exclusions and from current law. Apply the agency questions to every affected person as an explicit methodological choice.

Optional national, political, ideological, and religious lenses can reveal different consequences and priorities. Keep their baseline reference, sources, assumptions, and changed inputs visible. The two prototypes demonstrate separate scores without altering the baseline. They are not installed app plugins or endorsements by the represented communities.
